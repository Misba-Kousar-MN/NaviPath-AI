# Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) Overview

Official Source: Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Government of Karnataka
Authority: Karnataka BOCW Welfare Board

Source IDs: src-005, src-006

## 1. Statutory Mandate and Board Overview
The Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) was established in 2007 under the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 and the Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers Rules, 2006. The Board is responsible for administering the statutory welfare fund raised via construction cess and disbursing social welfare assistance to registered construction workers across Karnataka.

## 2. Beneficiary Registration Requirements
- Eligible Trade: Any building or other construction worker engaged in building construction, masonry, carpentry, bar bending, plumbing, electrical installation, painting, demolition, or earthwork within Karnataka.
- Minimum Employment: The worker must have worked for not less than 90 days as a construction worker during the preceding 12 months.
- Age: The applicant must have completed 18 years of age and not reached 60 years of age.
- Required Application Documents: Form V application, Aadhaar card, employer certification / 90-day work certificate (certified by registered contractor, trade union, or labour officer), and active bank account passbook.

## 3. Welfare Entitlements and Support Programs
Registered construction workers holding a valid Board identity card are eligible for various welfare assistance schemes:
- Educational Assistance: Annual scholarships for children of registered construction workers studying in primary, secondary, pre-university, undergraduate, and technical/diploma institutions.
- Toolkits and Equipment: Distribution of safety kits and trade-specific toolkit assistance to enhance on-site safety and technical productivity.
- Marriage Assistance: Financial grant disbursed for the marriage of registered construction workers or their children.
- Maternity Benefit: Financial assistance provided to registered female construction workers during pregnancy and childbirth.
- Accident Relief and Funeral Expenses: Immediate financial relief in the event of accidental death or permanent disablement occurring during construction employment.
- Official Helpline: Labour helpline 155214 is operational for registered worker queries and application status tracking. Note: Exact cash assistance figures are subject to state gazette notifications and require official source verification before disbursement citations.
