## 1. National Skill Training Institutes (NSTI) in Karnataka
The Directorate General of Training directly operates apex National Skill Training Institutes in Karnataka to deliver advanced Crafts Instructor Training Scheme (CITS) and specialized Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS) courses:

### 1.1 National Skill Training Institute (NSTI) Bengaluru
- Official Address: Outer Ring Road, Near Peenya Metro Station, Yeswanthpur Suburb, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560022. (Also referenced at Tumkur Road, Bengaluru).
- Primary Disciplines: Advanced welding technologies, computer numerical control (CNC) machining, tool and die making, electrical installation and maintenance, IoT and smart manufacturing.
- Program Levels: Crafts Instructor Training Scheme (CITS) for training future ITI instructors; advanced CTS programs for high-skill vocational trades.

### 1.2 National Skill Training Institute for Women (NSTI-W) Bengaluru
- Official Address: Hosur Road, Near Dairy Circle, Lakkasandra / Hombegowda Nagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560029.
- Focus Areas: Women-focused vocational skilling in architectural draftsmanship, computer aided design (CAD), digital applications, fashion technology, and modern IoT-driven electronics.
- Infrastructure: Specialized laboratory facilities, women's hostel, incubation facilities, and technical placement tie-ups.
