## 1. Qualification Brief and Job Role Overview
A Self Employed Tailor operates independently to design, cut, stitch, finish, and repair bespoke garments based on customer specifications and body measurements.
- QP Code: AMH/Q1947, Version 2.0
- NCO Code: NCO-2015/7531.0100 (Tailors and Dressmakers)
- NSQF Level: Level 4
- Minimum Entry Age: 18 Years
- Minimum Educational Qualification: 8th Class pass with 6 months of relevant apparel sector experience, OR 10th Class pass with no prior experience required.
- Training Duration: Typically 300 to 350 hours covering classroom theory, garment lab practicals, entrepreneurship, and soft skills.

## 2. Key National Occupational Standards (NOS) & Competencies
The qualification mandates mastery over five core competencies:
1. Client Consultation and Measurement (AMH/N1947): Taking accurate body measurements, documenting customer preferences, suggesting fabric types, estimating yardage, calculating cost quotes, and scheduling delivery dates.
2. Pattern Drafting and Fabric Cutting (AMH/N1948): Drafting paper patterns for basic and contemporary garments (blouses, kurtas, shirts, trousers, dresses), placing patterns efficiently on fabric to minimize wastage, and cutting panels with industrial cutting shears.
3. Sewing and Assembly Operations (AMH/N1949): Operating manual, motorized, and single-needle lockstitch industrial sewing machines; assembling collars, sleeves, cuffs, pockets, plackets, waistbands, and zippers.
4. Garment Finishing and Quality Inspection (AMH/N1950): Trimming loose threads, pressing and ironing garments, attaching fasteners (hooks, buttons, snaps), checking seam allowances, and conducting final fit inspections.
5. Workshop Safety and Machine Maintenance: Routine cleaning, needle replacement, oiling, bobbin tension adjustment, and adherence to electrical and ergonomic workplace safety standards.

## 3. Entrepreneurial Outcomes and Industry Pathways
Graduates possess the technical and business skills necessary to establish proprietary tailoring micro-enterprises, join boutique design studios, or work as sample tailors in export garment manufacturing factories in Karnataka apparel clusters (e.g. Peenya, Bommanahalli, Doddaballapura).
