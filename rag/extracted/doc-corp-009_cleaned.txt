# Qualification Pack — Mechanic Motor Vehicle (ASDC Standards)

Official Source: Automotive Skills Development Council (ASDC) / DGT CTS
Authority: ASDC / NCVET / DGT
Course ID: crs-008
Skill ID: skl-010
Source ID: src-026

## 1. Trade Profile and Qualification Overview
The Mechanic Motor Vehicle trade qualifies technicians to perform complete maintenance, diagnostics, servicing, and overhauling of internal combustion engines, commercial vehicles, passenger cars, and motorized two/three-wheelers.
- NSQF Level: Level 4
- Duration: 2 Years under CTS (or 400-500 hours modular QP for Automotive Service Technician).
- Entry Qualification: Passed 10th Class examination with Science and Mathematics.
- Minimum Age: 14 years (CTS) / 18 years (QP employment).

## 2. Key Diagnostic & Servicing Competencies
- Engine Systems: Disassembly, inspection, measurement, and reassembly of petrol and diesel engine components (cylinder block, piston rings, crankshaft, camshaft, valves, timing gears/belts).
- Fuel and Exhaust Systems: Servicing Common Rail Direct Injection (CRDI) diesel systems, electronic fuel injection (EFI) petrol systems, turbochargers, intercoolers, catalytic converters, and diesel particulate filters (DPF).
- Transmission and Drivetrain: Inspection and repair of manual and automatic gearboxes, clutch assemblies, propeller shafts, universal joints, differential units, and drive axles.
- Braking, Steering, and Suspension: Overhauling hydraulic, vacuum-assisted, and air brake systems; Anti-lock Braking System (ABS) diagnostics; wheel alignment and balancing; power steering racks; McPherson strut suspension.
- Auto Electrical and OBD Diagnostics: Scanning on-board vehicle electronic control units (ECUs) using OBD-II diagnostic scanners, reading fault codes (DTCs), troubleshooting starter motors, alternators, battery management, lighting, and wiring harnesses.

## 3. Workshop Safety and Career Progression
Certified technicians find direct employment in authorized automobile dealership service centres, state road transport corporation workshops (KSRTC, BMTC), logistics fleet maintenance yards, and independent automotive repair garages.
