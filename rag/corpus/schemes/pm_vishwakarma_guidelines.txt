# PM Vishwakarma Scheme Guidelines

Official Source: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India
Authority: Ministry of MSME, MoSD&E, and Department of Financial Services
Portal URL: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/
Source ID: src-001

## 1. Scheme Overview and Objectives
PM Vishwakarma is a Central Sector Scheme launched to provide end-to-end support to artisans and craftspeople who work with their hands and tools. The scheme aims to:
- Enable the recognition of artisans and craftspeople as Vishwakarmas, making them eligible to avail all the benefits under the Scheme.
- Help them upgrade their skills and provide them with relevant and suitable training opportunities.
- Support them with better and modern tools to enhance their capability, productivity, and quality of products.
- Provide them with access to collateral-free credit and reduce the cost of credit by providing interest subvention.
- Provide them with incentives for digital transactions to encourage digital empowerment.
- Provide them with a platform for brand promotion and market linkages.

## 2. Eligible Traditional Trades (18 Trades)
The scheme covers artisans and craftspeople engaged in 18 traditional family-based trades:
1. Carpenter (Suthar)
2. Boat Maker
3. Armourer
4. Blacksmith (Lohar)
5. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
6. Locksmith
7. Sculptor (Moortikar, stone carver), Stone breaker
8. Goldsmith (Sonar)
9. Potter (Kumhaar)
10. Cobbler (Charmakar) / Shoesmith / Footwear artisan
11. Masons (Raajmistri)
12. Basket / Mat / Broom Maker / Coir Weaver
13. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
14. Barber (Naai)
15. Garland maker (Malakaar)
16. Washerman (Dhobi)
17. Tailor (Darzi)
18. Fishing Net Maker

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools and engaged in one of the 18 family-based traditional trades mentioned above in the unorganised sector on self-employment basis.
- The minimum age of the beneficiary should be 18 years on the date of registration.
- The beneficiary should be actively engaged in the concerned trade on the date of registration and should not have availed credit-based schemes of the Central Government or State Government for self-employment or business development (e.g. PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, Mudra) in the past 5 years.
- The registration and benefits under the Scheme shall be restricted to one member of the family. For the purpose of the Scheme, a 'family' is defined as consisting of the husband, wife, and unmarried children.
- A person in government service and their family members shall not be eligible under the Scheme.

## 4. Scheme Benefits and Interventions
The scheme provides five major types of benefits to registered Vishwakarmas:

### Recognition
- Recognition through PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card acknowledging their traditional skills.

### Skill Upgradation
- Basic Training of 5 to 7 days (40 hours) with a daily training stipend of Rs 500 per day.
- Advanced Training of 15 days (120 hours) or more for interested candidates, with training stipend of Rs 500 per day.

### Toolkit Incentive
- A toolkit incentive grant of up to Rs 15,000 provided in the form of e-vouchers / digital voucher upon commencement of Basic Training.

### Credit Support (Enterprise Development Loans)
- Collateral-free Enterprise Development Loans:
  - First Tranche: Up to Rs 1,00,000 (repayable over 18 months).
  - Second Tranche: Up to Rs 2,00,000 (repayable over 30 months) for beneficiaries who have availed the first tranche, maintained a standard repayment record, adopted digital transactions, or completed advanced skill training.
- Concessional Interest Rate: A concessional interest rate of 5% is charged from the beneficiary, with an interest subvention cap of 8% paid by the Government of India.
- Credit Guarantee fees are fully borne by the Government of India.

### Incentive for Digital Transactions
- An incentive of Re 1 per digital transaction for a maximum of up to 100 transactions per month is credited to the beneficiary's bank account.

### Marketing Support
- Marketing assistance in the form of quality certification, branding, onboarding on e-commerce platforms such as GeM, advertising, and trade fairs.

## 5. Verification Process and Documentation
Registration is conducted through Common Service Centres (CSCs) via biometric authentication on the PM Vishwakarma Portal.
Required verification documents:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory identity proof).
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar.
- Active Bank Account details (Bank Account number, IFSC code).
- Ration Card / Family Declaration.
Verification is conducted through a three-stage process:
1. Stage 1: Gram Panchayat / Urban Local Body (ULB) verification.
2. Stage 2: District Implementation Committee (DIC) screening.
3. Stage 3: National Steering Committee approval.
